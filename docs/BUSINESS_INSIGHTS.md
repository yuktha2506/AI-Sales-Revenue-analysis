# Business Insights

The platform generates decision-oriented insights from actual sales data.

## Amazon Sale Report Snapshot

After excluding cancelled, zero-quantity, missing, and invalid amount rows, the included Amazon Sale Report contains 108,071 clean sales rows, 100,727 unique orders, and INR 71.67M revenue across March-June 2022.

## Top Revenue Drivers

Category-wise and product-wise revenue show which SKUs deserve inventory priority, paid campaign budget, and merchandising space.

In the dataset, `Set` is the largest category at about INR 35.73M, followed by `kurta` at INR 19.43M and `Western Dress` at INR 10.21M. The top SKU by revenue is `J0230-SKD-M`, followed closely by `JNE3797-KR-L`.

## Seasonal Trends

Monthly revenue trends reveal spikes and dips. A dip can trigger investigation into out-of-stock products, cancelled orders, shipping issues, or weak promotions.

Revenue peaks in April 2022 at about INR 26.23M, then softens to INR 23.95M in May and INR 21.39M in June. That pattern suggests the business should review campaign intensity, inventory availability, and fulfilment quality after April.

## High-Performing Categories

The top category chart helps teams decide where to expand assortment and where to protect supply.

## Forecasting Decisions

The next 3-6 month forecast gives a baseline for revenue planning. It helps finance estimate expected sales and helps operations prepare stock.

## AI Assistant

The assistant translates KPI and trend data into plain-English answers. It is grounded with summary statistics, so it can explain questions like "Why did sales drop last month?" using the latest month, previous month, category mix, and top products.
